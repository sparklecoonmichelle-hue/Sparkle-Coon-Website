Frequently Asked Questions

Some of the questions we are most commonly asked about our Maine Coons and kittens.

Have a Question?

We know that welcoming a Maine Coon into your family is a big decision. Here are some of the questions we receive most often.

Click on a question below to reveal the answer.

What is the price?

Our kitten prices are provided to anyone that contacts us. We do not publish our prices publicly on the website.

Do you have kittens now / available?

Kitten availability changes throughout the year. We do not always have kittens available, and upcoming litters are not necessarily available to everyone.

Information about suitable kittens and upcoming availability is shared with approved applicants.

How do I get a kitten?

Our kitten process begins with an application. Please contact us through WhatsApp for more information.

Applications are reviewed and suitable families are considered when kittens become available. We then work with approved families to find the right kitten match.

We prefer families to meet their kitten in person before making their final decision. If an in-person visit is not possible, a video call is required.

Do you have a waiting list?

We do not use a traditional waiting list. Instead, we use an application process.

Applications are opened periodically and are valid for six months (May be extended upon request). Approved applicants can then be contacted when a suitable kitten becomes available.

How big do Maine Coons get?

Maine Coons are one of the largest domestic cat breeds. Adult cats can vary considerably in size, with adults generally weighing around 4–10 kg depending on the individual cat.

Maine Coons are also a slow-maturing breed and may continue developing for several years.

What is the size difference between a female and male?

Male Maine Coons are generally larger and heavier than females, although there is considerable variation between individual cats.

A female can still grow into a large Maine Coon, while some males may be smaller than expected. Size should therefore not be judged by sex alone.

Do Maine Coons get along with kids and other animals?

Maine Coons are generally known for being social and adaptable cats, and many do well with children and other animals.

However, every cat has its own personality. Introductions should always be done gradually and with consideration for the individual animals involved.

Are Maine Coons affectionate?

Yes! Maine Coons are often affectionate and enjoy spending time with their families.

However, affection can look different from cat to cat. Some Maine Coons are very people-oriented and enjoy lots of attention, while others prefer to stay close to you without necessarily sitting on your lap.

Can Maine Coons walk on a leash?

Yes, Maine Coons can be trained to walk on a leash. Their intelligence and curious nature can make them suitable for leash training.

Training should be introduced gradually and positively, using a properly fitted harness and allowing the cat to become comfortable with it first.

Not every cat will enjoy leash walking, so it is important to follow the individual cat's comfort level.
