Meet the young cats who may become part of the future of the Sparkle Coon breeding program.

NEXT GENERATION

Sparkle Coon's Echo

Echo is quite possibly the most mischievous little character in our cattery. She has a knack for finding trouble wherever it may be hiding and seems to believe that anything left on the counter is fair game — especially bread! Despite her mischievous nature, Echo is also a sweet and affectionate girl who enjoys attention, although she is equally happy doing her own thing when she feels like it. She is confident, curious, and certainly not afraid to get involved. We suspect Echo is going to keep us entertained for many years to come as she grows into her role as a future queen.

Colour: Black Classic Tabby

Paws: Standard

Parents: CruSaders Luna Phantom

Sparkle Coon's Mystique

Mystique is a more reserved little girl who tends to be selective about the people she gives her affection to. She has a particularly strong preference for Michelle and is much more comfortable with her than with most others. Like her mom Ava, Mystique has inherited a love for fresh tap water and will happily enjoy a drink straight from the tap. She also has a rather cheeky habit of claiming the best possible spot — the top of whichever cushion you happen to be sleeping on! Mystique may be reserved, but she certainly knows what she wants and has her own quiet little personality

Colour: Black Silver Tortoiseshell Tabby (High Grade Silver)

Paws: Polydactyl

Parents: Sparkle Coon's Ava
GoodHeart Nurgani

NEXT GENERATION

Sparkle Coon's Snow White

Snow White is a reasonably confident girl who likes to be where the action is. She particularly enjoys her morning attention and is often happy to come looking for some affection at the start of the day. She is also very curious about what is happening around her and likes to inspect any new projects in the garden — especially when we are busy building or putting something together. She also has a rather adorable habit of exposing her belly, often leaving us with what can only be described as a very fluffy woolly cloth! Snow White has a lovely combination of confidence, curiosity, and a playful little personality that makes her fun to have around.

Colour: Black Silver Mackerel Tabby with White

Paws: Polydactyl

Parents: Sparkle Coon's Pearl (Retired)
GoodHeart Nurgani
