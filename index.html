Quick Introduction

We are a family-run, non-show Maine Coon cattery with South African, Polish, and Russian lines, specializing in a wide range of colours and the polydactyly trait.

We have chosen not to participate in cat shows, allowing us to dedicate more time to our cats and breeding program while keeping their lives as relaxed and stress-free as possible.

We are proud to preserve the naturally occurring polydactyl trait. While polydactyl Maine Coons are not eligible to compete in the show ring, the trait is naturally occurring within the breed, and we are pleased to offer these unique cats to loving homes.
