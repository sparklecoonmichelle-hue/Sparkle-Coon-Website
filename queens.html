Our Queens

Meet the beautiful ladies who are currently part of the Sparkle Coon breeding program.

CURRENT QUEEN

Sparkle Coon's Reese

Reese is an affectionate yet independent girl who enjoys attention, but is also perfectly happy doing her own thing. She has a lovely balance between wanting to be involved and knowing when she would rather have some quiet time to herself. Reese can be quite people-oriented, especially when she is in the mood for company, and she has a particularly fun love of water. Whether it is investigating a dripping tap or simply getting involved with anything water-related, her fascination with water is definitely one of her little quirks.

Colour: Black Tortoiseshell Tabby Bicolour (Calico)

Paws: Polydactyl

CURRENT QUEEN

Sparkle Coon's Tayla

Tayla is a playful and affectionate girl who enjoys attention and loves being involved in whatever is happening around her. She particularly enjoys going for walks and exploring her surroundings. Tayla is also very food motivated — perhaps a little too motivated! She knows how to sit and even turn on command, although she is quick to remind us that tricks are much more enjoyable when there is a treat involved. She is a bright and fun-loving girl with plenty of personality.

Colour: Blue Classic Tabby

Paws: Polydactyl

CURRENT QUEEN

Sparkle Coon's Silvy

Silvy is a sweet and affectionate girl who genuinely enjoys being around people. She loves attention and has a gentle, loving nature. One of her sweetest little habits is giving hugs — when she is in the mood, she will happily snuggle in close and show her affection. Silvy has a warm and people-loving personality that makes her a very special girl in our home.

Colour: Black Silver Ticked Tabby (High Grade Silver)

Paws: Standard

CURRENT QUEEN

Sparkle Coon's Ava

Ava is a calm and easy-going girl who also has a playful and energetic side that comes out when she feels like it. She particularly enjoys her time in the garden and has a funny habit of rolling around in the dirt — apparently, garden time is also the perfect opportunity to get a little messy! Ava is also a big fan of fresh tap water and will happily drink straight from the tap whenever given the chance. She has a sweet, relaxed personality with plenty of little quirks that make her uniquely Ava.

Colour: Blue Silver Tortoiseshell Tabby Bicolour (Diluted Silver Calico)

Paws: Polydactyl

CURRENT QUEEN

Sparkle Coon's Lexi

Lexi is an incredibly affectionate and people-loving girl who absolutely craves attention. She loves being around people and has no problem making sure you know when she wants some attention. One of her signature habits is giving you a little head bonk whenever you walk past her — apparently, simply passing by is an invitation for a cuddle! Lexi is a very social girl who loves interacting with people and is always happy to receive a little extra affection.

Colour: Black Mackerel Tabby (Golden)

Paws: Polydactyl

CURRENT QUEEN

Sparkle Coon's Jade

Jade is a calm and affectionate girl who enjoys attention, but is equally happy having some time to herself. She is a bit of an introvert and can be reserved around strangers, preferring to observe from a comfortable distance until she gets to know someone. Once she is comfortable, her affectionate side comes through. Jade also has a love for high places and can often be found perched somewhere up high, quietly watching everything happening around her. She has a gentle, peaceful personality and very much enjoys having her own little space.

Colour: Black Tortoiseshell Tabby with white (Calico)

Paws: Polydactyl

CURRENT QUEEN

CruSaders Luna Phantom

Luna, whom we affectionately call Luni, came to us from CruSaders Cattery, a new cattery that we are mentoring. From the moment she arrived, it was clear that Luni is not a girl who likes to sit on the sidelines. She is incredibly brave, curious, and always wants to be right in the middle of whatever is happening. Whether we are busy planting, working with tools, or simply getting things done around the house, Luni wants to investigate and be part of the action. Nothing seems to faze her — she approaches new things with confidence and curiosity. At CruSaders, she earned the nickname “Loonitunes” because of her tendency to suddenly take off running around like a little whirlwind. Since we have already had a Luna, we decided to give her the shorter nickname “Luni”, which suits her energetic personality perfectly. She is a wonderfully bold and entertaining little girl with a personality that is impossible to miss.

Colour: Black Bicolour

Paws: Standard

CURRENT QUEEN

CruSaders Coco Chanel

Coco is Luni's sister and our young up-and-coming queen, with her first litter expected around September–October. 👀😉 She is a very social girl who loves being involved and is certainly not shy about letting us know what she thinks. Coco is quite the little chatterbox and has a habit of talking — and occasionally complaining — about absolutely everything!

Colour: Black Silver Classic Tabby

Paws: Polydactyl
